DISEASE: Tomato Leaf Mold
CROP: Tomato
SCIENTIFIC / COMMON NAME: Tomato leaf mold

SYMPTOMS:
Tomato leaf mold commonly begins as pale green or yellow areas on the upper leaf surface. Corresponding areas on the underside may develop olive-green to brown or grayish fungal growth. Severely affected leaves may yellow and drop.

FAVORABLE CONDITIONS:
High humidity, poor air circulation, and prolonged leaf wetness favor disease development.

MANAGEMENT:
Remove severely affected leaves where practical. Improve air circulation and plant spacing. Avoid unnecessary leaf wetness. Maintain field sanitation and monitor the crop regularly.

PREVENTION:
Use healthy planting material. Provide adequate spacing and airflow. Avoid excessive irrigation that keeps foliage wet. Maintain sanitation and remove infected debris where appropriate.

MONITORING:
Inspect both upper and lower leaf surfaces for yellow patches and characteristic fungal growth, especially during humid conditions.

FARMER ACTION:
Remove severely affected leaves where practical, improve airflow, reduce unnecessary leaf wetness, and consult local agricultural experts if symptoms continue to spread.

IMPORTANT NOTE:
Chemical control recommendations should follow pesticide labels and local agricultural authority recommendations.
