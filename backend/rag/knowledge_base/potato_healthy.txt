DISEASE: Potato Healthy
CROP: Potato
SCIENTIFIC / COMMON NAME: Healthy potato plant

SYMPTOMS:
A healthy potato plant generally has normally developed green leaves without characteristic disease lesions, widespread yellowing, unusual spots, or abnormal drying.

FAVORABLE CONDITIONS:
Healthy growth is supported by suitable temperature, adequate soil moisture, balanced nutrition, sufficient sunlight, good drainage, and appropriate crop management.

MANAGEMENT:
Continue regular crop monitoring. Maintain suitable irrigation and nutrition according to local recommendations. Keep the field clean and provide adequate spacing and airflow.

PREVENTION:
Use healthy planting material. Maintain appropriate plant spacing. Avoid excessive irrigation. Maintain field sanitation and monitor plants regularly.

MONITORING:
Inspect leaves and stems regularly for new spots, lesions, yellowing, wilting, or abnormal growth.

FARMER ACTION:
If the crop appears healthy, continue routine monitoring and good agricultural practices. If unusual symptoms appear, photograph the affected plant and seek appropriate agricultural advice.

IMPORTANT NOTE:
A healthy classification does not guarantee that a plant is free from every possible pest or disease.
