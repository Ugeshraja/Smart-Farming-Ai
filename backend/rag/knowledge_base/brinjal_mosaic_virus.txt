Brinjal Mosaic Virus Management (TNAU & ICAR Agronomic Compendium)
====================================================================

Causal Pathogen:
Brinjal Mosaic Virus / Tobacco Mosaic Virus / Cucumber Mosaic Virus strains.

Insect Vector:
Cotton / Melon Aphid (Aphis gossypii) and green peach aphid (Myzus persicae) in non-persistent stylet-borne manner.

Diagnostic Symptoms:
- Pronounced mosaic pattern of alternating light-yellowish green and dark-green patches on leaf blades.
- Upward leaf puckering, blistering, surface rugosity, and vein banding.
- Significant reduction in plant vigor, stunting, distorted flower buds, and mottled small fruits.

Favorable Conditions:
- High aphid vector populations during warm, dry spells following monsoon.
- Presence of alternate solanaceous weed hosts harboring viral reservoirs.

Integrated Vector & Virus Management:
1. Field Sanitation & Cultural Measures:
   - Carefully rogue out and burn or bury infected plants immediately to curb sap/vector transmission.
   - Maintain a weed-free zone around the field perimeter, eradicating Solanum nigrum and Physalis minima.
   - Wash farm tools and hands with soap/trisodium phosphate after handling foliage to prevent mechanical transfer.

2. Aphid Vector Management:
   - Install bright yellow sticky traps @ 15-20 traps per acre to monitor and capture flying aphids.
   - Apply Neem Oil 1% (10 ml/L) or NSKE 5% early in the season.
   - Chemical spray: Apply systemic insecticide Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 0.3 g/L or Acetamiprid 20% SP @ 0.2 g/L or Dimethoate 30% EC @ 1.5 ml/L.

3. Monitoring:
   - Scout field borders and leaf undersides weekly for aphid colonies.
