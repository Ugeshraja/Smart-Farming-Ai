Brinjal Bacterial Blight Management (TNAU & ICAR Agronomic Compendium)
========================================================================

Causal Organism:
Pseudomonas / Xanthomonas species affecting Solanum melongena.

Diagnostic Symptoms:
- Water-soaked circular to irregular dark brown lesions beginning at leaf tips and margins.
- Rapid marginal necrosis and inward leaf scorching under warm, humid conditions.
- Blighted leaves turn brittle, roll inward, and drop prematurely.

Favorable Conditions:
- High relative humidity (>80%) and temperatures between 26°C and 32°C.
- Heavy rainfall, splashing water droplets, and leaf injuries.

Integrated Disease Management:
1. Cultural Practices:
   - Provide proper drainage channels to avoid standing water.
   - Destroy and burn infected foliage residues after harvest.
   - Maintain crop rotation with non-solanaceous crops (e.g., maize, pulses).

2. Chemical & Bactericidal Controls:
   - Spray Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L combined with Streptocycline @ 100 ppm (1 g in 10 L water) at the initial appearance of leaf lesions.
   - Repeat spray at 10-12 day intervals if weather conditions remain humid.

3. Preventative Bio-control:
   - Foliar application of Pseudomonas fluorescens (5 g/L) or Bacillus subtilis (5 g/L) at transplanting and early vegetative growth.
