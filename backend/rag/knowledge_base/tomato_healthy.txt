DISEASE: Tomato Healthy
CROP: Tomato
SCIENTIFIC / COMMON NAME: Healthy tomato plant

SYMPTOMS:
A healthy tomato plant generally has normally developed green leaves without characteristic disease lesions, widespread yellowing, abnormal curling, severe pest damage, or unusual drying.

FAVORABLE CONDITIONS:
Healthy growth is supported by suitable temperature, adequate soil moisture, balanced nutrition, sufficient sunlight, good drainage, and appropriate crop management.

MANAGEMENT:
Continue regular crop monitoring. Maintain suitable irrigation, nutrition, spacing, and field sanitation according to local recommendations.

PREVENTION:
Use healthy planting material. Maintain proper spacing and airflow. Avoid excessive irrigation. Maintain field sanitation and inspect plants regularly.

MONITORING:
Inspect leaves, stems, flowers, and fruit regularly for spots, lesions, yellowing, curling, wilting, pests, or unusual growth.

FARMER ACTION:
If the plant appears healthy, continue normal crop care and monitoring. If new symptoms appear, photograph the affected plant and seek agricultural advice.

IMPORTANT NOTE:
A healthy classification does not guarantee that a plant is free from every possible pest or disease.
