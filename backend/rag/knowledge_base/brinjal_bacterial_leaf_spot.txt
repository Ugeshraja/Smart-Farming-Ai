Brinjal Bacterial Leaf Spot Management (TNAU & ICAR Agronomic Compendium)
===========================================================================

Causal Organism:
Xanthomonas campestris pv. vesicatoria.

Diagnostic Symptoms:
- Small, dark brown to blackish water-soaked circular to angular lesions on foliage.
- Lesions are distinctly surrounded by bright yellow chlorotic halos.
- Advanced infection leads to severe defoliation, reducing photosynthesis and fruit size.

Favorable Conditions:
- Warm weather (25-30°C) accompanied by high humidity, fog, or overhead sprinkler irrigation.
- Wind-driven rain and mechanical transmission via handling wet foliage.

Integrated Disease Management:
1. Agronomic Measures:
   - Avoid overhead sprinkler irrigation; adopt drip irrigation to keep leaf canopy dry.
   - Space plants adequately (60 x 60 cm or 75 x 60 cm) to ensure good aeration.
   - Rogue out severely infected lower leaves touching damp soil.

2. Bactericidal & Protective Sprays:
   - Apply Copper Hydroxide 53.8% DF @ 2 g/L or Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L mixed with Streptocycline @ 0.1 g/L.
   - Alternate with Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2 g/L to prevent secondary fungal invasions.

3. Monitoring & Prevention:
   - Inspect lower canopy weekly during rainy periods.
   - Treat seeds with hot water (50°C for 25 minutes) or Streptocycline solution before nursery sowing.
