DISEASE: Tomato Yellow Leaf Curl Virus
CROP: Tomato
SCIENTIFIC / COMMON NAME: Tomato yellow leaf curl virus

SYMPTOMS:
Infected tomato plants may show upward curling of leaves, yellowing, reduced leaf size, shortened internodes, and stunted growth. Young plants can be strongly affected.

FAVORABLE CONDITIONS:
Spread is associated with infected planting material and insect vectors, particularly whiteflies. Conditions that favor vector populations can increase disease spread.

MANAGEMENT:
Remove severely affected plants where practical to reduce sources of infection. Monitor and manage insect vectors according to local recommendations. Maintain field sanitation and use healthy planting material.

PREVENTION:
Use healthy planting material. Monitor crops for vector insects. Remove infected plants where appropriate. Maintain field hygiene and follow locally recommended vector-management practices.

MONITORING:
Inspect young leaves for curling, yellowing, reduced leaf size, and stunted growth. Check plants for whitefly activity.

FARMER ACTION:
Separate or remove severely affected plants where practical, inspect nearby plants, monitor vectors, and consult local agricultural experts.

IMPORTANT NOTE:
Vector-control recommendations should follow pesticide labels and local agricultural authority guidance.
