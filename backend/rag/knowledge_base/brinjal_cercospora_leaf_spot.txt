Brinjal Cercospora Leaf Spot Management (TNAU & ICAR Agronomic Compendium)
===========================================================================

Causal Organism:
Cercospora melongenae (Foliar fungal pathogen).

Diagnostic Symptoms:
- Circular to irregular chlorotic spots on leaves that develop grayish-white centers with dark brown borders.
- Concentric ring-like appearance in older spots; severely affected leaves turn yellow, dry up, and fall off prematurely.
- Reduces photosynthetic leaf area and causes sunscald on unprotected developing fruits.

Favorable Conditions:
- Warm, humid weather (25-32°C) with persistent night dew and frequent rainfall.
- Overcrowded crop canopy and poor weed management.

Integrated Disease Management:
1. Agronomic Measures:
   - Remove and destroy infected lower leaves at first sight.
   - Maintain clean weeding and prevent foliage contact with soil.
   - Adopt drip irrigation to prevent water splashing onto foliage.

2. Fungicidal Spray Schedule:
   - Spray Carbendazim 12% + Mancozeb 63% WP @ 2 g/L or Chlorothalonil 75% WP @ 2 g/L upon notice of early leaf spots.
   - If disease pressure is high, alternate with Azoxystrobin 23% SC @ 1 ml/L or Difenoconazole 25% EC @ 0.5 ml/L at 14-day intervals.

3. Monitoring:
   - Scout lower leaf canopy twice a week after rains.
