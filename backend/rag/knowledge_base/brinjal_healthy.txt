Healthy Brinjal Crop Care & Maintenance (TNAU & ICAR Agronomic Compendium)
==========================================================================

Agronomic Profile:
Solanum melongena healthy vegetative and reproductive canopy standards.

Visual Health Characteristics:
- Rich, uniform deep-green leaf blades free from chlorotic mottling, water-soaked lesions, necrotic spots, or upward curling.
- Vigorous stem branching and sturdy internodes capable of supporting heavy fruit load.
- Abundant flowering with clean violet/purple petals and healthy yellow stamens without bud drop.

Optimal Agronomic Schedule:
1. Balanced Nutrition (TNAU Recommendations):
   - NPK Application: 100 kg Nitrogen, 50 kg Phosphorus, 50 kg Potassium per hectare.
   - Basal: 50% N + 100% P + 100% K at land preparation along with 25 tonnes/ha FYM compost.
   - Top Dressing: Split remaining 50% Nitrogen equally at 30 days and 45 days after transplanting.
   - Foliar Micronutrient: Spray TNAU Brinjal Special or 19:19:19 water-soluble NPK @ 5 g/L during active flowering.

2. Irrigation Management:
   - Provide drip irrigation at 3-day intervals during vegetative stage and 2-day intervals during fruit set.
   - Maintain root-zone moisture at 60-65% field capacity. Avoid water stagnation.

3. Natural Plant Immunity Boosters:
   - Spray Panchagavya 3% (30 ml/L) or Vermiwash (50 ml/L) at 20-day intervals.
