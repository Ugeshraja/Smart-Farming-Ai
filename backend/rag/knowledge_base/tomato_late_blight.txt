DISEASE: Tomato Late Blight
CROP: Tomato
SCIENTIFIC / COMMON NAME: Late blight of tomato

SYMPTOMS:
Tomato late blight can cause dark green, brown, or black water-soaked lesions on leaves. Lesions may expand rapidly. Severely affected foliage can brown, collapse, and die. Fruit may also develop dark lesions.

FAVORABLE CONDITIONS:
Cool to mild temperatures, high humidity, rainfall, and prolonged leaf wetness favor disease development.

MANAGEMENT:
Remove severely affected plant material where practical. Maintain field sanitation. Improve airflow through appropriate spacing. Avoid unnecessary leaf wetness. Monitor the crop closely during wet weather and follow local disease-management recommendations.

PREVENTION:
Use healthy planting material. Maintain proper plant spacing. Avoid unnecessary overhead irrigation. Remove infected plant debris where appropriate.

MONITORING:
Inspect plants frequently after rainfall and during humid conditions for rapidly expanding dark or water-soaked lesions.

FARMER ACTION:
Inspect nearby plants, remove severely affected material where practical, improve sanitation and airflow, and consult local agricultural experts.

IMPORTANT NOTE:
Chemical control recommendations should follow pesticide labels and local agricultural authority guidance.
