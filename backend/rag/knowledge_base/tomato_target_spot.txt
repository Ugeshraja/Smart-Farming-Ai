DISEASE: Tomato Target Spot
CROP: Tomato
SCIENTIFIC / COMMON NAME: Target spot of tomato

SYMPTOMS:
Target spot can produce circular brown lesions on leaves. Lesions may develop concentric rings that create a target-like pattern. Severe infection can cause yellowing and premature leaf loss. Fruit lesions may also occur.

FAVORABLE CONDITIONS:
Warm, humid conditions and prolonged leaf wetness can favor disease development.

MANAGEMENT:
Remove severely affected plant material where practical. Improve plant spacing and airflow. Avoid unnecessary leaf wetness. Maintain field sanitation and monitor disease progression.

PREVENTION:
Use healthy planting material. Maintain suitable spacing. Manage irrigation to reduce prolonged foliage wetness. Remove infected debris where appropriate.

MONITORING:
Inspect leaves regularly for circular brown lesions and concentric ring patterns.

FARMER ACTION:
Remove severely affected leaves where practical, improve sanitation and airflow, and consult local agricultural experts if symptoms spread.

IMPORTANT NOTE:
Chemical control recommendations should follow pesticide labels and local agricultural authority recommendations.
